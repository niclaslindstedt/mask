# A PDF opens as the PDF

A document you upload is read into text — that is the text the detectors search,
the text the placeholders are woven into, and the text you hand to a model. But
a PDF is not text. It is a layout: a two-column judgment, a table of amounts, a
letterhead, a stamp in the margin, a signature at the bottom. Read it back as
paragraphs and all of that is gone, and with it your ability to check that you
are masking the document you think you are.

So a PDF now opens as the PDF. Press a document's name in the list — or **Read
source** above the review — and its own pages are drawn, one under the other,
fitted to the width you are reading at. The **+** and **−** buttons zoom, and
the percentage between them puts it back to fitting the width; the pages
themselves are drawn as you scroll to them, so a long document opens as quickly
as a short one.

**Text** beside **Document** switches to the extracted text, unmarked and with
its copy button. That is worth a look of its own: it is what the detectors read,
so a name the reflow ran together with the word after it is a name the detectors
may not find.

Anything that isn't a PDF — a pasted note, a text file — opens as its text, as
it always has.

## Where the file lives

Nowhere but your device. The PDF is kept in this browser so its pages can be
drawn again the next time you open the document, exactly as your projects and
their text already are. It is never uploaded, never sent anywhere, and Settings
→ Developer → **Erase all local data** erases it along with everything else. A
document whose file isn't kept — one added before this, or one in a browser that
won't give the app storage — opens as its text instead.
