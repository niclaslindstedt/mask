type: Added
title: Pinch the pages, and read either view full screen
doc: pdf-pages
---

Reading a source document, a PDF now pinches to zoom about the point between your fingers (or Ctrl and the wheel on a desktop), out past fitting the width until a whole page fits on a phone — and a button in the header takes either the pages or the text over the whole screen, putting you back where you were reading when you leave it.
