type: Added
title: Mask, always mask, or never mask the text you mark
doc: rules
---

Marking text in a document's preview now offers all three decisions at once, right above the document: **Mask** adds it to this review, **Always mask** adds it and blacklists it for every project, **Never mask** whitelists it so no detector flags it again. The last two are the shorthand for adding a value and then hunting down its row to press the list button.
