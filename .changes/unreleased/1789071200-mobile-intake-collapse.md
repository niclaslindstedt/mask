type: Changed
title: The intake gets out of the way on a phone
---

At phone width the document list and the review now share one scroll instead of two, so the list scrolls away as you move into the review — and the intake box above them folds into a single row the moment you scroll past it, giving the review that height back without putting the way to add a document out of reach. Scroll back to the top and it unfolds.
