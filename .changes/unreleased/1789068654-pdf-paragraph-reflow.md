title: PDF text keeps its paragraphs
---

A PDF used to arrive cut into one line per printed row — sentences split mid-clause, words split at their hyphens, page headers dropped into the middle of a paragraph; text now comes out as the paragraphs it was written as, with wrapped lines rejoined, hyphenated words put back together, running headers left out, and footers read in their place on the page.
